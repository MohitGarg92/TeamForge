TeamForge — Intelligent Team Formation Platform

Find your people. Build your team. Create something great.

TeamForge is a student-exclusive platform designed for Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), helping students discover teammates for hackathons, academic projects, and collaborative competitions.

Features

* Student Authentication — Designed for secure, student-restricted access.
* Team Creation — Create teams with defined member requirements and skills.
* Smart Discovery — Explore opportunities and filter teams by hackathons and skills.
* Join Requests — Request membership and let team leaders manage applications.
* Student Profiles — Showcase your resume, skills, GitHub, and LinkedIn.
* Automated Team Management — Prevent overfilling and transition completed teams to private status.

Tech Stack

HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · Node.js · Express.js · MySQL

Project Status

Currently under development. The frontend uses a localStorage-based simulation while backend integration and authentication enhancements are planned.

Our Goal

Make team formation simpler, more transparent, and accessible across the Thapar student community.
