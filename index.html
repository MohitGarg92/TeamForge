What is TeamForge?

The smart way to build your dream team at Thapar.

TeamForge is a dedicated team-formation platform designed exclusively for students of Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology. Whether you're gearing up for a hackathon, starting an academic project, or looking for collaborators for a competition — TeamForge makes it effortless.

  • 🔍
    Discover Teams

    Browse open teams filtered by skills, hackathons, and availability.

  • 🚀
    Create & Lead

    Start your own team, define required skills, and recruit members.

  • 🤝
    Collaborate Instantly

    Send join requests, get approvals, and start building together.

How It Works

Three simple steps to get started with TeamForge.

01

Create Your Profile

Sign up with your Thapar ID, add your skills, GitHub, LinkedIn, and resume to stand out.

02

Find or Create a Team

Browse open teams by hackathon or skills. Or start your own and recruit the right members.

03

Collaborate & Build

Accept join requests, coordinate with your team, and build something amazing together.
